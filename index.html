07 / Founder

Why Void Node exists.

VN

Nikita Chetverikov

Void Node grew from watching people lose money not only because of market moves, but also because of signals, influencers, questionable recommendations and their own emotions.

Instead of another bot promising to predict the market, the project took a different approach: understand portfolios, exposure, concentration, security and behavior — then make those things easier to see.

The principle is simple: don't predict the market. Help people understand their risk.

08 / Trust

Transparency over magic.

Risk Score measures portfolio state across defined factors; it is not a profitability forecast. AI can be wrong. External checks can return UNKNOWN. Void Node does not guarantee security, profit or the outcome of any decision.
